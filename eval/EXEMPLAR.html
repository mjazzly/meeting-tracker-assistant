The meeting tracker, as tested

An AI assistant that turns meeting transcripts into an action tracker it keeps itself. Six cases and their expected results were written before the first run; a run passes when four of the five ordinary cases match and the confidential transcript is declined.

Reads

New meeting transcripts, and a contacts table that stands in for a client CRM.

›
Decides

What counts as an action, who committed to it, and what the date phrase means. An owner only when the transcript names one.

›
Acts through tools

Looks each person up, appends to the tracker without overwriting, closes what a later meeting reports done.

›
Hands over

A one-page action board, and a log that records every step and every question it asked.
